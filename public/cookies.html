Cookie Policy

Last updated 23 September 2026

  • FactClash does not use cookies: no advertising cookies, no third-party cookies, no tracking cookies.
  • The app keeps a few settings on your device so the game works. That is why there is no cookie banner.
  • You can switch off anonymous usage statistics in your profile.

1. Cookies

We do not set cookies for players. Everything the site needs, including fonts and scripts, is served from factclash.com, so no other company's content loads on the page.

2. What the app keeps on your device

The app uses your browser's local storage for a few things you ask it to remember. They stay on your device until you clear the site's data.

NameWhat it holdsWhy
kc:profileYour nickname, level, game stats, a random ID, and whether usage statistics are onRemembers you between games and keeps your Daily Clash streak
kc:sessionThe room you are inLets you get back into your game after a reload
kc:solo:…Your Daily Clash and challenge progressLets you continue where you left off
kc:audio, kc:last…Your sound setting and last game settingsSaves you setting them again

These are strictly necessary for the game you asked to play, so the law does not require a consent banner for them.

3. Usage statistics

To improve the game, the page sends us anonymous usage information, such as which screens were used and whether a game was finished. This uses no cookies. To link one visit to the next, it uses a one-way scramble of the random ID above, so we cannot see the ID itself.

To switch this off, open your profile (tap your level badge) and turn off Help improve FactClash. It is also off automatically if your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal. See our Privacy Policy for details.

4. Clearing it

You can delete everything FactClash keeps on your device at any time by clearing the site's data in your browser settings. You will lose your level, stats and Daily streak on that device.
